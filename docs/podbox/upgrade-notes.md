- The carousel draws shadows now: one on the ground below the covers and, in both views, one each cover casts on the covers behind it. Both are on by default; **Floor Shadow** and **Cover Shadow** in the carousel's Appearance settings set how dark they are, or turn them off. **Background** also has a new choice, **Blurred Art**, the playing album's cover blurred behind the covers.
- The covers either side of the middle one are now slightly blurred, and the angled covers' stepped edges are smoothed. **Side Blur** (default 3, 0 is Off) and **Smooth Edges** (default 2 px, 0 is Off) are in the same settings. **Vignette**, off by default, darkens the screen's edges as a cheaper alternative to the shadows, and **Shadow Fade-In** sets how long the middle cover's shadows take to appear.
